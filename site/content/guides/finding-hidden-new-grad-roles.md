The board's rules are strict on purpose, and strict rules have misses. A "Software Engineer" at a startup with no level in the title, a role posted 70 days ago that is still open, a "Rust Engineer" whose description never says "new grad". Two features let you look past the rules without the board pretending those roles meet them.

## WIDEN SCOPE

Under the filters is a group of toggles. Each one adds back postings that failed exactly one soft rule:

- **internships & co-ops**: internships, co-ops, student placements and summer programs
- **level III+**: titles at level III or higher
- **outside US / CA / IN**: roles located in other countries
- **posted 60–120 days ago**: roles older than the 60-day window but not yet 120 days

A row appears only when every rule it fails is toggled on, so an internship in Germany needs both toggles. These rows are tagged with the reason, they are never counted in the board's numbers, and they are never in the feeds. The near-miss set is capped, so on a busy week the oldest ones drop off first.

## EXPLORE

The EXPLORE tab is different in kind. It loads a compact index of every unique posting the last run fetched from every source, most of which are not new grad jobs at all, and lets you filter it with your own signals. The layout mirrors HIRING: a rail of filters, a list, a detail pane.

- **INCLUDE chips** add words a title must contain: new grad, entry level, junior, associate, early career, level I / II, L3 / L4, a start year, graduate program, "0–2 years". Type your own too.
- **EXCLUDE chips** remove titles with senior, staff, principal, lead, manager, director, architect, intern, co-op or "5+ years".
- **PRESETS** are saved combinations. "Unlevelled software titles" is the one most people want: plain "Software Engineer" titles with no seniority word.
- **TIER** shows whether a row is on the board, a near miss, or out, and the detail pane explains which rule decided it and which of your include words matched.
- **POSTED, COUNTRY, SOURCE and ROLE** narrow further, and the result count updates as you go.
- **EXPORT CSV** downloads the current result for a spreadsheet.

Signals match whole words, the same way the scraper's rules do: "intern" does not match "international". Matching is on titles, because the index carries no descriptions. And the whole state lives in the URL, so a good query is a link you can keep.

## What to do with what you find

An EXPLORE result is a lead, not a verdict. Open the posting and read the requirements with [Are you qualified?](../are-you-qualified/) in mind. Unlevelled startup titles often turn out to be mid-level, and a rule-free view will show you those alongside the real openings. That is the trade: more coverage, more reading.

## When to use which

Use WIDEN SCOPE when you want the board's judgment with one rule relaxed: you are open to co-ops, or you will relocate abroad. Use EXPLORE when you disagree with the rules, or when you are hunting in a niche (a language, a domain, a company) where the entry-level signal is often missing from the title.
